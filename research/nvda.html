Thesis

I am buying NVIDIA at a split adjusted $17.55, in anticipation of the market realizing how AI infrastructure is one of the biggest investment trends for the next several years to decade. NVIDIA is the source of most chips used in training AI models, with their CUDA software forming the foundation of current machine learning research, making it incredibly difficult to move away from despite other available chips on paper. The price is not cheap, as I'm buying it at around 100x earnings, but I believe that in a year when revenue is flat and earnings dropped by over 50% due to writedowns of inventory in the gaming graphics card segment due to lower demand after the pandemic (most likely explained by the decrease in working from home and leisure time), the fundamentals are still incredibly strong, with gross margins staying near 57% and return on equity of around 19% in the same period, showing that NVIDIA is a fundamentally strong company with growth potential that is currently being hampered by a temporary downturn in demand in one segment. I have continued to hold on to this stock, and it has been the single strongest position in my portfolio, as the competitive advantage holds strong, being incredibly difficult to overcome despite the rise of other chipmakers with considerable funding backing them. Each step in the adoption of new AI methods and technologies required more and more computing power, and NVIDIA has been able to retain its position as the primary supplier for both chips and software for each new generation of technology. I have not sold any shares as the position grew in size as a large portion of my portfolio due to the overall rise of the position, as I believe that the overall trend has a long way to go before peaking, and I did not want to cap my gains on a stock that has proven incredibly difficult to find a way out for those looking to short the long position due to its fundamentals and network effects. I keep a close eye on potential competition and the risk of revenue concentration on a few large customers, but none of it has cooled my overall enthusiasm for the stock.
